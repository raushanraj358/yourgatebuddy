Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can use YourGATEBuddy as a guest or with an account. This page explains what we store in each case.

Guest use

Your profile (name, GATE paper, target year, exam date, daily goal), syllabus checklist, quiz history, saved questions, activity log, reminders and settings are saved in your browser’s local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.

With an account

If you sign in (with Google or email and password), we use Google Firebase Authentication to manage your login. The same study data listed above is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account, so it can sync across your devices. Only you can read or write it. Your Claude API key is never uploaded. Signing out removes your data from that device; it stays in your account.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset all permanently deletes your study data from your account and the device. You can also export a backup first. To delete your login itself, contact us.

AI quizzes

If you add a Claude API key, it is saved only in your browser. When you generate an AI quiz, your chosen paper, subject, topic and difficulty are sent with your key directly to Anthropic’s API (api.anthropic.com) to create questions. Anthropic’s own privacy policy covers that request. No personal profile data is included.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are created by your browser on your device. Calendar files (.ics) are generated locally and downloaded to your device.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Accounts and synced data are processed by Google Firebase. If you host this site with analytics, update this policy to describe it.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner through yourgatebuddy.com.
