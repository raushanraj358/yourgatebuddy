Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

YourGATEBuddy is built to keep your data on your own device. We do not require an account and we do not run servers that store your study data.

What is stored

Your profile (name, GATE paper, target year, exam date, daily goal), syllabus checklist, quiz history, reminders and settings are saved in your browser’s local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them. You can export a backup or reset everything from Settings.

AI quizzes

If you add a Claude API key, it is saved only in your browser. When you generate an AI quiz, your chosen paper, subject, topic and difficulty are sent with your key directly to Anthropic’s API (api.anthropic.com) to create questions. Anthropic’s own privacy policy covers that request. No personal profile data is included.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are created by your browser on your device. Calendar files (.ics) are generated locally and downloaded to your device.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. If you host this site with analytics, update this policy to describe it.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner through yourgatebuddy.com.
